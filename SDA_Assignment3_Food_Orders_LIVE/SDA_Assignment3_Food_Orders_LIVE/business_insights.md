# Business Insights

The live dashboard provides managers with continuously refreshed visibility into food-order activity. Order count and revenue are updated whenever a new simulated event arrives, allowing changes in workload and sales to be observed without waiting for a batch report. The order-status chart separates confirmed, preparing, pending, out-for-delivery, and delivered events, which can help identify where current orders are concentrated in the fulfillment process. Revenue by restaurant provides a comparative view of sales contribution across restaurants. Payment-method analysis shows how customers use UPI, cards, wallets, and cash on delivery. The dashboard also displays revenue over time and a latest-events table, allowing recent activity to be inspected immediately. These insights are descriptive for the simulated dataset; a real business would combine them with historical data, delivery-time measures, cancellations, and service-level metrics before making operational decisions.
